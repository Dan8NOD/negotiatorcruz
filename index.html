The Cruz Protocol

Four stages. Run in order. Every time.

Motivation is a mood — it decays in about 72 hours. A protocol is infrastructure: your team runs it on a Tuesday when the deal is bad and nobody feels inspired. That's the whole difference.

Stage 01
Read

Diagnose the emotional state before a single ask leaves your mouth. You can't steer a car you haven't looked at.

Emotion Wheel · Labels · Silence
Stage 02
Disarm

Say the worst thing they're thinking before they have to. Resistance you name out loud stops being ammunition.

Accusation Audit · Mirrors
Stage 03
Steer

Move them with questions instead of pressure. "How" and "What" do the work "Why" can't.

Calibrated Questions · Summaries
Stage 04
Close

Let them talk themselves into it, then stop talking. The close is mostly restraint — and nobody trains restraint.

No-Oriented Questions · Silence
