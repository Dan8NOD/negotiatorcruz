Things worth knowing

How fast do you reply?
Usually within a day. I'm on TikTok Live two hours a day Monday to Saturday, so if it's urgent, the call booking is faster than the form.
Do you travel?
Yes — based in Chicago, travel billed at cost. Virtual sessions are available for the Negotiator Hour and keynotes; the intensives work far better in person because the roleplay rounds need a room.
Will you tell me not to book you?
Regularly. If your team hasn't opened the free app and your problem is motivation rather than method, a training day is the wrong purchase and I'd rather say so on a 15-minute call than after you've paid.
Can you do a pilot before we commit the whole team?
Yes. A common route is a Negotiator Hour with the sales lead first, then a one-day for the floor if it lands. Costs $500 to find out instead of $5,000.
