Field card 25 of 33. One More Thing. Part IV. CLOSE.

Before you lock it, ask what's missing. The ask you invite is cheap. The ask you suppress is expensive.

Window.
After "yes," before signature. Requires a "that's right" first (Ch 22).

The Sweep.
"Before we sign, what did I miss that would make this better?" "Before we call it done, is there a loose end we haven't tied off?"

Sharpest.
"What's the thing you almost brought up but didn't?" Presupposes it exists. There always is one.

Safest.
"If this deal could include one more thing at no extra cost, what would you ask for?" Scopes the answer before they give it.

The Feel.
"Is there something small that would make this feel like a win, not just a deal?"

Never Ask.
"Are you happy?" Everyone says yes.

Rules.
Hold six seconds after. Know your small change BEFORE you ask. Grant it or trade for it. Never deflect.

Scored.
Sweep on every deal. items surfaced. granted or traded. cost tracked. 30-day friction falling

Fails When.
You ask, they answer, and you flinch. Now they've learned asking you is unpleasant, for the length of the contract.

Recover.
"I asked that like a formality and I actually mean it. What's the thing you almost brought up and didn't?"
