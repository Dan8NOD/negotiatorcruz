Field card 2 of 33. The Four Stages. Part 0. STANDARD.

READ, DISARM, STEER, CLOSE. In order. Every time.

1 Read.
Diagnose. Emotion + decision structure.

Cleared.
Name 1 specific emotion + 3 participants.

2 Disarm.
Say the worst thing before they do.

Cleared.
Said it, didn't defend, temperature fell.

3 Steer.
Hand them the problem. How / What.

Cleared.
They propose something. Anything.

4 Close.
Summarize. Wait. Stop talking.

Cleared.
"That's right." (not "you're right")

Scored.
Sequence intact. Read ≥25% of call. Disarm present. Steer talk time <40%. Zero concessions after silence

Fails When.
You jump to Steer because it's the stage that feels like negotiating.

Recover.
"I've been throwing solutions at this and I haven't understood it. What's making this harder than it needs to be?"
