Field card 30 of 33. When The Protocol Fails. Part V. APPLICATIONS.

Three conditions defeat the method. Trying harder makes all three worse.

Bad Faith.
No intention of agreeing.

Tell.
Enthusiasm, no process detail. Nothing you concede produces movement.

Test.
Ask for a small commitment. A next step with a named attendee. They won't give it.

No.
They cannot agree, whatever they say.

Authority.
Pronouns drift to "they." Everything is

Tell.
provisional.

Test.
Ask for a decision with a date. Authority gives a mechanism; absence gives a feeling.

Deadlock.
Both right, incompatible. Rarest.

Tell.
Constraint is specific, consistent, and external. Survives probing.

Test.
Probe it (Ch 19). Bracket it. If it holds, it's real. Say so out loud.

End It.
"Sounds like that's a no." "It looks like there's real pressure on you to end this one way or another." A third of the time it reopens honestly.

Before You.
1 What if I negotiate? 2 What if I don't?

Continue.
3 Does this violate anything I hold? 4 Who else is affected?

Scored.
Diagnosis at 3 stalls. commitment test run. time-to-kill tracked. clean endings

Fails When.
You try harder. Sustained attention on someone who can't be helped reads as pressure however well executed.

Recover.
"Is there a version of this that works for you, or should I stop?"
