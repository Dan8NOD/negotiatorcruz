Field card 23 of 33. The Fairness Standard. Part IV. CLOSE.

Hand them a permanent right to stop you. Deals die from UNSTATED objections, not stated ones.

The Offer.
"Stop me the moment this stops feeling fair to you." "If something feels off, I'd rather hear it now than after." "Say the word and we'll rebalance this on the spot."

Who.
"Fair doesn't mean equal, tell me what

Defines.
fair looks like to you." Most fairness fights are definitional.

Autonomy.
"You don't have to accept something just because I proposed it." "If this ever feels one-sided, that's on me to fix, not you to absorb."

Not This.
"We're just asking you to be fair." That's the weapon version. Works once, visible to anyone who's seen it.

Rules.
Deliver EARLY, before terms. Only say it if you mean it. Honour rate must be 100%.

Scored.
Delivered before terms. times used. 100% honoured. fair defined in their words. late-stage surprises falling

Fails When.
They use it and you argue. Now nothing you offer carries weight.

Recover.
"I just did the exact thing I told you I wouldn't. You said it feels one-sided, what would balanced look like?"
