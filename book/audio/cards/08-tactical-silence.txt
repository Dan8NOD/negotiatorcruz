Field card 8 of 33. Tactical Silence. Part I. READ.

Silence isn't empty. It's still your turn. You just haven't used it yet.

Use It.
After a label. After your number. After a calibrated question. When they've gone quiet and are working.

Don'T.
As punishment. After a direct question. On a bad video connection.

The Hold.
Six seconds. Count them. Breathe out. Don't summarize what you just said. It already landed.

The Voice.
Drop pitch. Slow down. Trail sentences DOWN, not up. Say the hard part in the same tone as the easy part. Match energy, not volume. Rushing a number makes it negotiable.

Scored.
6-second hold. they speak first >50%. ZERO concessions after silence. zero sentences after your own price

Fails When.
You read quiet as displeasure and improve an offer nobody objected to.

Recover.
Make it a trade, not a gift: "I meant it, if the start date moves to the first."
