Field card 12 of 33. The Power Of "No". Part II. DISARM.

"No" is the beginning. Go and get it on purpose. People agree to things they're free to refuse.

Invert.
"Do you have a few minutes?"
, "Is this a bad time?"
"Are you still interested?"
, "Have you given up on this?"
"Am I understanding correctly?"
, "Is my read on this wrong?"

Surface.
"Is something being left unsaid?" "Would you be against being honest here?" "Are you against putting everything on the table?"

Stalls.
"Should I just stop trying?" "Is this hopeless, or am I missing something?"

Check Self.
"Have I misunderstood what you need?" "Am I talking to the wrong person?"

Rules.
Only ask what you can survive a "yes" to. Never re-ask the same one. Ask it, then SILENCE. No "because." No "or."

Scored.
Orientation ratio. early no obtained. all asks survivable. zero re-asks. 30-day stalls converted either way

Fails When.
You fear the no you asked for and hand them three exits.

Recover.
"Sorry. I'll just ask it straight. Is this a bad time?"
