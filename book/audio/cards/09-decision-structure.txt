Field card 9 of 33. Decision Structure. Part I. READ.

The person in front of you is usually not the person you're negotiating with.

Pronouns.
"I", real ownership
"We", a committee exists. Find it.
"They", authority is elsewhere
A name, gold. Write it down.
A switch mid-sentence, their real limit

Info Label.
"It seems like there's a detail nobody's said out loud yet." "It looks like I need help with this." POWER LABEL "It sounds like whoever's really in charge isn't even in this room." "It seems like you'd need permission just to say yes to this." Flat and warm. Never sharp. Confirm OR pushback. Both are wins.

Mislabel.
"It looks like this decision is entirely yours to make." Must be PLAUSIBLE. Aim just past what you believe.

Scored.
3+ named participants. signer confirmed not assumed. >50% facts on the map. 1+ structure probe per discovery call

Fails When.
You believe the enthusiastic contact.

Recover.
"You've been carrying this internally and I don't want that to fall on you alone. Who else should be in the next one?"
