Field card 13 of 33. Validation. Part II. DISARM.

Their position makes sense from where they stand. That is NOT agreement. You concede nothing.

Empathy.
"That's not an overreaction. That's what this situation calls for." "Anyone in your position would be asking the same questions right now." "It makes sense you'd want more than my word on this."

Positive.
"It seems like you'd like to make this work, but there are things in the way." "You seem like the type of person who prides themselves on how they do business." Must be TRUE. Untrue = flattery = costly.

Trust.
"At what point did trust break down here?" "What's the smallest thing I could do right now that would mean something?" "What's the fastest way I could lose whatever trust we've built so far?"

Rules.
Never follow with "but." Validate, then be QUIET. Under 90 seconds, it's a bridge, not a destination.

Scored.
Validated + terms unmoved. zero "but". label passes the truth test. defending stopped

Fails When.
You validate, then argue in the same breath. Now they know what it precedes.

Recover.
"I did that badly. I validated you and then argued with you. Let me leave the first part where it is."
