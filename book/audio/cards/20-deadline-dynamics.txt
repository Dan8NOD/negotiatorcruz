Field card 20 of 33. Deadline Dynamics. Part III. STEER.

Most deadlines aren't real. The real ones usually belong to someone who isn't in the room.

Test It.
"What actually breaks if this takes one more week?" "Is there a real consequence to missing this date, or just discomfort?" Real deadlines have MECHANISMS. Fake ones have adjectives.

Name It.
"It seems like the deadline is doing more negotiating than either of us." WHOSE? "Whose deadline is this really, yours, or someone you're trying not to disappoint?"

Loss.
"What's already slipping away while this sits unresolved?" "What's the first thing you lose that you can't get back?" ALWAYS a question. Never a warning. A loss they calculate can't be disputed.

Scored.
100% of deadlines tested. zero concessions to an untested date. zero loss delivered as a statement. cost of inaction in THEIR words

Fails When.
You repeat their date back as a shared constraint without ever asking what it is.

Recover.
"Before I go rearranging things around the 15th, what actually happens on the 16th?"
