Field card 10 of 33. The Accusation Audit. Part II. DISARM.

Say the worst thing they think, before they can. An objection you've already voiced is spent.

Build It.
"You probably think…" "It might seem like…" "It probably looks like…"

Motive.
"You probably think I'm just trying to squeeze you here." CREDIBILITY "You might be thinking I don't actually know what I'm talking about."

After.
"You might be thinking I'll disappear the second this is signed."

Rules.
Rank by DAMAGE, not likelihood. Open with the top two. Say it and STOP. No "but." No defense. Flat tone, same as the easy part.

Listen For.
A laugh, pressure released
"Yeah, honestly", the real objection
They upgrade it, better than being right
They defend YOU, now they're consistent

Scored.
Present before the ask. top-two severity. ZERO defense sentences. room cooled

Fails When.
You audit the safe one and leave the real objection sitting there.

Recover.
"That wasn't the real one. The real one is that you think the number is high and you've been burned before."
