Field card 5 of 33. The Emotion Vocabulary. Part I. READ.

Get the STATE right before you get specific.

Torn.
Two good reasons, opposite directions.
, Name the tension, don't argue a side.
"You're not indecisive, you're being honest about the tradeoffs."

Ready.
Thinking's done. Waiting on logistics.
, Stop selling. Start scheduling.
"Let's put a real date on it. Not soon, not this week. A date."

Not Yet.
No problem, so no negotiation.
, Find the future where they'd have one.
"Is this a someday thing, or a never-really thing?"

Somatic.
Say what you SAW, never what it means. "Your jaw just tightened. That's not agreement." Max one per ten minutes.

Scored.
Specific not generic ("frustrated" = 0). evidence on every read. state called right. Ready spotted before the last call

Fails When.
Pressure collapses you back to the same three adjectives.

Recover.
"Something's landing harder than the rest and I don't want to guess. What is it?"
