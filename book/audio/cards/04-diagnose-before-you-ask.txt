Field card 4 of 33. Diagnose Before You Ask. Part I. READ.

Output: you can state their position better than they did. Not kindly. Accurately.

Encouragers.
"Mm-hmm.". "Go on.". "And then what?" "Say more about that.". "Take your time." The rule is they END. Then you stop.

Listen For.
"It sounds like the surface ask isn't the real ask." "Is this a won't, or is it a can't?" "What are you actually protecting here?" "If I only understood one thing about this, what should it be?"

Clean Lang.
Repeat THEIR word. Make it smaller. "Complicated", complicated how? "Expensive", compared to what? "Risky", risky how, specifically? Never swap in your own word.

Scored.
Talk time <30%. half the fuzzy words made specific. playback needs no amending. zero solutions offered

Fails When.
You listen in order to reply. They can hear it. They start giving you the short version of everything.

Recover.
"I got ahead of you there. Go back, you were saying something about ___."
