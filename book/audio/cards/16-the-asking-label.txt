Field card 16 of 33. The Asking Label. Part III. STEER.

Get the answer without asking the question. A question tells them what you're worried about. A label doesn't, and gets a fuller answer.

Instead Of.
"Are you talking to other vendors?"

Say.
"It sounds like you're comparing this against something else you're weighing."

Instead Of.
"What's your budget?"

Say.
"It seems like there's a number in mind, even if it hasn't been said yet."

Instead Of.
"Do you need approval for this?"

Say.
"It seems like this needs a yes from someone else in the room first." THE BIG ONE "It seems like you'd say yes today if the one thing holding you back got solved."

Off Decoy.
"It sounds like the real sticking point isn't the one we're talking about."

Rules.
No question mark. Ever. Aim for ~70% right, corrections carry more detail than confirmations. One per exchange. Then six seconds.

Scored.
Intelligence gained without disclosure. correction rate >60%. silence held. one per exchange

Fails When.
You panic in the silence and append the question anyway. No mid-call recovery. Drill the six seconds instead.
