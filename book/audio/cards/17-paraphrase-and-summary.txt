Field card 17 of 33. Paraphrase And Summary. Part III. STEER.

Understanding is proved by reproduction, not by claiming it. "I hear you" is worth nothing.

Paraphrase.
One point, mid-conversation. "Let me say that back, and you tell me where I'm wrong." Shape it X-not-Y: "…the timing matters more than the number." "…this comes down to trust, not terms." Always paraphrase BEFORE you disagree.

Summary.
Everything, in THEIR words. THREE items. "So: you want speed, you need cover, and you've been burned before."

The Closer.
"Here's where we are: two things agreed, one thing still open. Let's look at that one." Banks the agreements. Isolates the problem.

Scored.
Summary present before Close. exactly 3. >70% their vocabulary. open item named. paraphrase precedes every disagreement

Warning.
"Yeah, sounds about right" = one of your three is wrong. Ask which.

Fails When.
You summarize your own pitch, or you drop the item you can't answer. They notice the omission every time.

Recover.
"That was my summary, not yours. Let me try again, everything YOU'VE told me."
