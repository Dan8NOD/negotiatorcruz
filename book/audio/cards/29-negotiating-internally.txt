Field card 29 of 33. Negotiating Internally. Part V. APPLICATIONS.

You cannot walk away. No BATNA changes everything. Optimize for the relationship. It produces every future outcome.

Up.
Audit first: "You're probably thinking I'm asking because my team is behind." Then: "What am I not seeing from where you sit?"

Sideways.
No authority, no exit. Trade. "If I take this on, what comes off my plate?" "What's cheap for you that would be expensive for me?" "It seems like you've been carrying more of this than anyone's acknowledged."

Down.
The trap is authority. Don't use it. "What's the part of this you disagree with?" ← presupposes it exists

Never.
Escalation as leverage. Works once, remembered permanently. Winning in front of an audience.

Scored.
Outcome AND relationship, separately. escalations near zero. peer asks paid for. disagreement surfaced before execution. audit used upward

Fails When.
You win the meeting. It reverses in two weeks through channels you can't see.

Recover.
"That went my way in the room and I don't think it landed well. What did I miss about your side of it?"
