Field card 7 of 33. Mirroring. Part I. READ.

Their last 1-3 words. Upward inflection. Then nothing.

Do.
"A budget issue this quarter?" "Not interested?" "Impossible?" "Sixty thousand?"

Rules.
Up, not flat, flat reads as disagreement. Nothing follows it. Not one word. Mirror the number, never your reaction. Best target = heaviest word, not last.

Don'T.
Twice in a row. Direct factual questions. Anything genuinely heavy (use a label).

Listen For.
They keep going, the point
Absolute, conditional, the gold
They self-correct, new information
They laugh, say "fair enough"

Scored.
Clean delivery. elaboration rate >70%. max 1 per 2 min. zero reaction leakage

Fails When.
It doesn't land so you do it again.

Recover.
Drop the tool. "Let me be more direct, what's actually driving that number?"
