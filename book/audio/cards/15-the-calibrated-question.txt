Field card 15 of 33. The Calibrated Question. Part III. STEER.

Hand them the problem. They own answers they make. Grammar: HOW and WHAT. Never WHY.

Pushback.
"How am I supposed to do that?" "What would need to be true for this to work?" "What's making this harder than it needs to be?"

Constraint.
"What about this is most important to you?" "What's the one piece you're least willing to bend on?" "How did you land on that number?"

Toward Yes.
"What would need to happen for this to be an easy yes?"

Build It.
"You need to…", "How am I supposed to…?"
"Why did you…?", "What made that the
right call?" Never answerable with yes or no.

Scored.
Talk time <40%. zero "why". all open. silence held. count THEIR proposals

Fails When.
You answer your own question because the pause got uncomfortable. Three seconds feels like thirty. Let it run.

Recover.
"I jumped in on my own question. Let me put it back to you, how would you want to handle it?"
