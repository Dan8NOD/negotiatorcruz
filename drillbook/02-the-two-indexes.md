## Index two: by what you have

Time and bodies, the two real constraints. Every drill in the book,
placed by what it actually needs.

| You have | Run |
|---|---|
| Ten minutes, two people | 07 |
| Fifteen minutes, two people | 11, 21, 25, 34, 35 |
| Fifteen minutes, three people | 08, 14 |
| Fifteen minutes, the whole room | 05, 06, 31, 33 |
| Fifteen to twenty minutes, solo at a desk | 12, 15, 16 |
| Twenty minutes, two people | 17, 19, 22, 24, 28, 29, 36 |
| Twenty minutes, three people | 04, 13 |
| Twenty minutes, the room | 01, 02, 10, 20, 37, 38, 39 |
| Twenty-five to thirty, the room with live pipeline | 09, 18, 27, 30 |
| Solo, on your own accounts, across days | 26 |
| A week of field time | 23 |
| Managers only | 32, and 33 to calibrate them |

Two placements worth explaining. Drill 23 is the only one that
cannot be compressed, because its scoring happens in the field a
week after its setup, and that gap is the drill. And Drill 31 is in
the whole-room row even though it is fifteen minutes, because it is
not a drill you run once. It is the room every other drill runs
inside, weekly, and the index lists it so nobody hunts for it.
