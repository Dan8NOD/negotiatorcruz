# Part V: The Programs

A single drill is useful. A sequence of drills with a calendar is a
curriculum, and a curriculum is a thing an organization runs without
anyone from Negotiators on Demand in the building. Three programs,
smallest first.

---

## The four-week install

This is the manual's 30-day rollout (The Cruz Protocol, Ch 31) with
this book's drills slotted in. The weekly rhythm is fixed: the Monday
Fifteen (Drill 31) opens the week, every rep records their next two
live calls Wednesday and Thursday, not their best two, and Friday
closes with two calls scored in front of the room on the twelve-row
scorecard (Drill 33).

| Week | Stage | Monday drill | Recorded metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | READ | 04, then 05 | Talk-time ratio |
| 2 | DISARM | 10, then 11 | Objections surfaced before the other side raised them |
| 3 | STEER | 15, then 19 | Questions asked before any number named |
| 4 | CLOSE | 08, then 21 | Concessions made after silence |

One tool per week. The temptation to compress this into two weeks is
strong and wrong, for the manual's stated reason: the constraint is
not how fast people learn a sentence. It is how long a sentence takes
to survive a bad call.

---

## The twelve-week curriculum

All thirty-eight drills, sequenced. Two rooms run in parallel: the
floor's weekly session, and the manager track that starts in week one
and never stops. Homework drills are solo and take fifteen minutes at
a desk.

| Week | Theme | Room drills | Homework |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the call | 02, 04 | record two live calls |
| 2 | The vocabulary | 05, 06 | 12 |
| 3 | Mirrors and silence | 07, 08 | 16 |
| 4 | The map | 01, 09, 36 | 26, if a renewal is inside ninety days |
| 5 | The audit | 10, 14 | 15 |
| 6 | The unsaid | 11, 13 | rerun 12 on live asks |
| 7 | Questions, not demands | 19, 35 | 20 |
| 8 | The currencies | 18, 24 | map one live account |
| 9 | The mandate and the path | 34, 37 | write both paths for one live deal |
| 10 | The close | 21, 22, 25 | hold one six-count on a real call |
| 11 | Live fire | 28, 38, and 23 begins its field week | 30, if the quarter allows |
| 12 | The lock and the loop | 39, 17, 29, 27 | 23 reports out |

**The manager track, alongside:** Drill 31 opens every week from week
one. Drill 33 closes every week from week two. Drill 32 runs monthly,
managers only, and no manager signs off a rep's level who has not
passed it. That last rule is what makes the certification ladder in
the manual's Chapter 2 mean something.

Week twelve is not an ending. The floor that finishes it re-enters at
the symptom index, running whatever the numbers say is weakest, which
is how a curriculum becomes an operating rhythm.
