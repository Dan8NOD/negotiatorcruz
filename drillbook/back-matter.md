## Where each drill points

Every drill practices a chapter. When a drill lands and the room
wants the doctrine underneath it, this is the map: Drills 01 to 33
carry their chapter numbers from *The Cruz Protocol*, there is no
Drill 03 because the manual's Chapter 3 has nothing to drill, and
Drills 34 to 39 practice the six steps of *Six Before Yes*, one
each, in order.

The deeper library, for the reader who wants it: the manual for the
full tool doctrine, the guide for the deal arc around the
conversation, and *Same Words, Bigger Rooms* for the same tools at
every table in a life.

## Also from Negotiators on Demand

*One method, four doors, and a place to practice it live.*

**The books.**

- *The Cruz Protocol.* The corporate field manual. Thirty-three tools in
  four stages, drilled and scored, for the person who negotiates for a
  living.
- *Six Before Yes.* The deal around the conversation. Six steps, three of
  them before contact, for the person who owns the whole deal.
- *The Drill Book.* The practice curriculum. Thirty-eight drills with
  setup, scoring, and what goes wrong, for the person running the room.
- *Same Words, Bigger Rooms.* The everyday book. The same tools at the
