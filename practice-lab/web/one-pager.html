per seat

A four-hour live, instructor-led session in which participants run mock negotiations against a trained counterpart and receive individual, recorded feedback on their performance.

What participants can do afterwards

  • Open a negotiation without conceding position — structure the first exchange so the opening number is not set by the other side.
  • Handle objections without discounting — respond to price and terms pressure using tested verbal technique rather than concession.
  • Use tactical empathy under pressure — labels, mirrors and calibrated questions, drilled to the point of recall in live conditions.
  • De-escalate a hostile counterparty — recognise and defuse adversarial dynamics before they cost the relationship or the deal.
  • Self-assess on tape — each participant leaves with a recorded round and a scored breakdown of what to change.
