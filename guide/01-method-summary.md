## SIX BEFORE YES
### The Negotiators on Demand deal method

> Most negotiations are lost outside the room. Six moves decide the outcome,
> and only one of them is a conversation.

| | Step | The core question | You're done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BEFORE** | **1 · Know Your Exit** | What am I authorized to do, and what happens if I walk? | You can state your walkaway and who holds the decision rights on your own side. |
| | **2 · Find the Stakes** | What is genuinely at stake here, and for whom? | You can name what each side stands to lose, including the things nobody says out loud. |
| | **3 · Map the Room** | Who decides, who influences, who can quietly kill this, and what state are they in? | Three named people, and a state call: Torn, Ready, or Not Yet. |
| **DURING** | **4 · Pick Your Path** | What is my path, my trade set, and my walk-away? | A chosen strategy, a currency map, and the number where you stop. |
| | **5 · Run the Protocol** | How do I create movement without spending credibility? | Read → Disarm → Steer → Close, run in order, ending on "that's right." |
| **AFTER** | **6 · Lock and Log** | How do I lock the agreement, and get smarter from it? | An agreement that survives implementation, and three sentences in the log. |

**The weighting is the argument.** Three of six steps happen before contact.
If you meet the other side at Step 1, you are not early. You are three steps
late.

**Step 6 points back at Step 1.** The log from this negotiation is the mandate
work of the next one. That loop, run weekly, is the entire difference between
ten years of experience and one year repeated ten times.
